# How a post passes review

Here is how a post passes review. Every post moves along a belt with three gates before it goes live.

The first gate is Screening, where software flags spam and unsafe words. About 88% of posts pass Screening.

The second gate is Review, where an editor checks facts and tone. Around 70% of the remaining posts pass Review.

The third gate is Approval, where the owner signs off and picks a time. Nearly 95% of reviewed posts pass Approval, and only then does the post go live.
