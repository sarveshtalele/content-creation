Reel looks and motion

Every Reel has a kinetic hook, masked scene reveals, a camera push, parallax, animated counters (10 and up) and particles on connectors. Choose the look:

  • lightClaude light (default): warm paper, clay accents
  • cinematicCinematic dark: dark grade, light sweeps, impact flash

Motion the decision matrix can pick for your content:

  • bar race with counters
  • bars grow along the time scale
  • bars step up and down to the total
  • branch highlight travels down the chosen path
  • camera pans along the timeline
  • card flip per resource
  • cone fills slice by slice; counters tick down
  • donut sweep; waffle cells fill in order
  • exploded parallax: layers pull apart
  • kinetic typography: the claim builds word by word
  • line draws with easing; end counter ticks up
  • network animation: links draw, nodes pop in, dots travel
  • node pulses travel the loop; decision diamond flips
  • particles flow along ribbons; ribbons draw in with easing
  • path tracing with staggered cards
  • radar draws axis by axis; heat cells fill
  • split screen, then morph A into B
  • typed terminal with cursor
  • zoom reveal into one branch

Opening visuals (Reel visual hooks):

  • Start with the result: Show the finished output immediately.
  • Open mid-action: Begin while the activity is already happening.
  • Screen recording: Show the software or workflow in action.
  • Before and after: Display two contrasting results immediately.
  • Zoom reveal: Start with a close-up and reveal the complete scene.
  • Unexpected visual: Open with something unusual but relevant.
  • Text-first: Show a strong headline while the visual demonstrates the subject.
  • Fast transitions: Connect multiple short clips to establish context.
  • Proof-first: Show a real screenshot, output or physical result.
  • Split screen: Compare two methods simultaneously.
  • Mistake demonstration: Show the wrong approach before explaining the correction.
  • Transformation: Show the process evolving into the final result.
