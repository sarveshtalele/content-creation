# Why most free trials end before data

Of 1,000 visitors who start on the pricing page, 72% never connect a data source. The trial funnel: visit pricing, start a trial, connect data, pay.
What blocks people: an empty dashboard, no sample data, a long setup. What gets them through: a sample workspace, one-click import, guided setup.
Trials, onboarding and retention depend on that first connection. Product teardown.
