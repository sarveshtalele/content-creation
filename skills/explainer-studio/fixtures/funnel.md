# Why most sign-ups never become customers

Of 1,000 people who visit the pricing page, 400 start a free trial. Of those, 240 connect their first data source, which is the step that predicts everything else. Only 96 reach a paid plan, and 72 are still paying after three months.

The biggest drop is between starting the trial and connecting a data source: 40% of trials end there. Fixing onboarding at that step does more than any pricing change.
